# ICP Source — "Best fit prospects" one-pager

Transcribed from the official DOSS sales-enablement one-pager provided by Max (2026-06-17). This is
the authoritative source for `config/icp.yaml`.

> A **physical or consumer goods (CPG) company** that is growing fast and drowning in manual
> processes. The chart gives a sense of best-fit companies for DOSS; there may be a fit outside
> these parameters — reach out to confirm.

## Best fit

| Dimension | Best Fit |
|-----------|----------|
| Revenue | $10M–$200M |
| Employees | 10–500 |
| Location | US / Canada |
| Products | Food & bev, beauty, supplements, home goods, pet, apparel, consumer electronics — anything with physical inventory |
| Manufacturing | Uses co-manufacturers and/or 3PLs (outsourced) |
| Current systems | QuickBooks + spreadsheets, or broken/failed tool stack |

\* Today we avoid prospects that need full ERP migration (NetSuite, Intacct, Microsoft, Epicor,
Acumatica, etc.) or complex WMS features/build-out.

## Key differentiators
- **Built for operations, not just accounting.** Most ERPs are finance-first. DOSS starts with the
  operational workflows that run the business — orders, inventory, procurement, fulfillment — and
  connects them to the GL, not the other way around.
- **Flexible, not rigid.** Unlike legacy ERPs that force predefined workflows, DOSS adapts to how a
  business actually operates. Process changes don't require a consultant or a multi-month implementation.
- **AI-native.** AI is built into the core for implementation acceleration, workflow automation, and
  operational intelligence — not bolted on after the fact.

## Disqualifying statements
- **No physical inventory** — pure software or services companies are not strong fits.
- **Heavy in-house manufacturing & warehousing** — DOSS focuses on businesses that mostly outsource
  production (co-mans/3PLs). Limited-to-no outsourcing is a poor fit today.
- **Government, pharmaceuticals/hospitals, real estate, restaurants, VC firms** — not served today
  (industries served will expand over time).

## Notes
- **Large companies:** don't turn away inbound $200M–$500M+ companies. Longer sales cycles, but we
  engage them — always send them our way.
- **Competition:** see https://www.doss.com/competitors for a comprehensive competitive overview.
